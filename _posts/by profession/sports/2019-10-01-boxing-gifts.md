We watch sports to marvel at the absolute potential of humans to master physical feats and compete to prove to be the best. But when it comes to boxing, it’s not just a sport about physical excellence, it’s about our need to watch violence that drives us. As the world as it is today, violence of any form is pretty much frowned upon. No one even likes war anymore. After all, with thousands of civilizations ending nukes in existence, it’s just better to be cautious and take a more diplomatic approach to doing things. But the primal need for violence remains in us.

To put it simply, it’s fun to watch dudes punching the crap out of other dudes. If you’re looking for a gift for a fighter, this list has something for all kinds of champions. I’d advise you against gifting equipment to professionals. They are picky about them and the best pair of punching gloves you give to them will likely end up in a box, eating dust.

Following is a list of the best gift ideas for fighters. Pick one that suits your recipient’s style.

That includes keeping track of their punches and trying to beat their own records.

It is mostly a collection of his interpretations of the writings of other great philosophers and his own ideas about combat and life.
