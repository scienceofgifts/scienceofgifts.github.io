How dull would music be without drums? You can’t rock the stage without a drummer pounding away like a victorious gorilla claiming its turf. From jazz legend Buddy Rich to Charlie Watts of [The Rolling Stones](/rolling-stones-gifts/ 'Rolling Stones Gifts'), drummers have shaped the evolution of music, their rhythms forming the backbone of every great song. Their contributions, alongside many others, are gifts to the art form.

Now, what do you get for someone who spends their days beating on things? Well, probably not drumsticks or new gear. Any seasoned drummer picks their own equipment, and they’re picky about it.
Sure, you might mean well when you follow those generic gift guides recommending drum accessories, but you know what they say about good intentions. Those gifts often end up collecting dust in the basement.

To save you from that fate, I’ve put together a list of gifts that drummers, whether aspiring or pro, will actually appreciate.
