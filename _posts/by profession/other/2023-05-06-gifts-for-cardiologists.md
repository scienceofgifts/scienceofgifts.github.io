Despite what the movies have led us to believe being a cop is not as fun and neither an easy job. It’s dull, filled with routine, mundane tasks, boring paperwork and involves dealing with annoying citizens who hate you. And even though it’s statistically safe to be a police officer, those numbers don’t help when dealing with dangerous situations with uncertain outcomes. 

Not all cops are good and virtuous. Some of them are bullies with a badge. It doesn’t mean all of them are bad though. Those who do put in the effort to maintain order in society deserve some appreciation.

The day one graduates from the police academy is filled with lots of hopes for good. For some getting that police badge is what they’ve always dreamed of and when that dream comes true, the joy is just too much to be kept to oneself. Be part of that joy, let them know you’re proud of their accomplishment. Following is a list of the best police academy graduation gift ideas. Pick one that suits your recipient.

The same as any other professional who takes pride in their work, police officers are proud of their title. It’s an important part of their identity and tied to their sense of worth. Which is why this police officer definition t-shirt will delight your recipient. 

The clever definition is written to appeal to a cop who believes in the work they’re doing. The design is simple, minimalistic yet creative so it’s more likely to impress a wide variety of people, so you’re less likely to get their style wrong.

This personalized police officer hanger is a good choice to give to a new officer on their graduation day. Seeing their name on the hanger will let them know that it’s made only for them.
