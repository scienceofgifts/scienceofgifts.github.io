You have likely heard the saying “Men are from Mars and women are from Venus.” And while nobody in their right mind believes that the two genders come from aliens from different places, most people can understand where the saying comes from. The genders are so different in the way we behave that it seems like we come from different planets. 

When it comes to gift exchange behavior these differences are quite noticeable. A woman is likely to get away with giving a coffee maker as a gift to your boyfriend, they might even appreciate the convenience it will bring them, but a guy doing the same will likely make the girlfriend drive into self doubt. “Does he even find me attractive anymore?” is what will likely go through her head. And don’t even get me started about giving oversized clothes to a girl. That’s the best way to end a relationship. 


As for picking a gift for a guy, if you are trying to make a good impression go for something handmade, even a DIY gift. If that’s not an option, following are some cool gift ideas men based on interests and personality. Pick one that suits your recipient best.
