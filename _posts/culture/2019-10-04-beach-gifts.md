I grew up in Goa. It’s the smallest state in India and is known for its tourist friendly beaches, spectacular nightlife and casinos. It’s like the Miami plus Las Vegas of the country. We get tourists from all over the world.

I'm not much of a gambler and I seldom go to these parties anymore but I do find myself going to the beach every now and then to enjoy the peace and the natural beauty of the places where the land meets the sea. For me the sound of water playing pull and push with the land is soothing and even comforting. It's like nature's own melody. 

Following is a list of the best gifts for people who love beaches. Pick one that suits your recipient.

You’re better off picking something that matches their general interests or relates to them. It also helps if they can put it use, even if the utility is looking nice on a wall. This exquisite seahorse wall decor is a fine gift idea for someone who like beaches.
