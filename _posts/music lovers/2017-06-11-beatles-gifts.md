Their journey to fame wasn’t instant. They spent years refining their sound, growing from four kids in Liverpool into a band that rewrote music history. They wrote songs that defined an era—songs of love, rebellion, war, and peace. Their influence wasn’t just musical; it was cultural. The Beatles didn’t just top charts—they changed the world.
